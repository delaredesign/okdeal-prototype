# OKDeal V1 specification

Positioning: Make the deal official.

Scope: work/service agreements only. Create → Review → Agree → Sign → Record.

## Required experience

1. Creator enters recipient name/email, optional phone, work title/description, amount/currency, start/completion dates, payment and additional terms.
2. Preview precedes creation. Creation generates an OKDeal ID and a high-entropy share token.
3. Recipient opens the link without registering an account, confirms the intended recipient and reviews the terms.
4. Recipient can request changes or accept and sign a specific version. Creator also signs that same version.
5. Both parties can see proposals and previous versions. A changed version requires fresh agreement and signatures.
6. Both signatures on the same version lock it and produce a completed record.
7. Creator dashboard and agreement detail show status, version history and activity.

## Rules

- Never overwrite a published version. Draft edits before first creation are not agreement revisions.
- Keep intended recipient details separate from the actual signer's declared name and verified email.
- A recipient identity mismatch stops the flow. The recipient cannot change the addressed identity.
- Email verification establishes control of an email address, not legal identity.
- Both parties have continuing access to the completed record.
- Do not collect IC/passport numbers, birth dates, bank details or other unnecessary sensitive data.
- Do not claim automatic legal enforceability or court-proof records.
